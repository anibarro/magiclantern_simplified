Live Composite LCM43 - Canon EOS M firmware 2.0.2

Olympus-style Live Composite for the EOS M at full sensor resolution
(5208x3478): the camera keeps exposing frames of the chosen shutter speed,
and every frame is merged into a composite that keeps the brightest value
of each pixel (light trails, light painting, star trails over a fixed
foreground). The result is an uncompressed RAW DNG at the card root.

INSTALL
Copy ML/modules/livecomp.mo to the same location on your ML card, replacing
only that module. Enable livecomp.mo in the Modules tab and reboot.
Keep your existing autoexec.bin and its matching EOSM_202.sym together.

CAMERA SETUP
 - Movie mode, M exposure, fixed ISO (not Auto; 100 recommended), fixed WB,
   normal view (x1). Camera on a tripod.
 - Aperture and ISO: set them with the camera's own controls before
   starting. They stay fixed for the whole session.
 - ML FPS override OFF, Global Draw OFF, no other RAW tools (crop_rec,
   mlv_lite, raw video, silent pictures).

MENU (Shoot > Live Composite)
   Live Composite   ON arms the module. Nothing starts until you press SET.
   Shutter          2 s ... 60 s (default 2.5 s): the exposure of every
                    frame; frames follow each other with no gap. The right
                    side shows the camera's ISO and aperture.
                      2 s: the LCD shows a quarter of the picture per frame.
                      2.5 s and longer: the whole picture after every frame.
                    2-5 s are camera-tested. 6-60 s are marked "not yet
                    tested" until a camera run confirms them.
                    Shorter shutters (Olympus goes down to 1/2 s) are not
                    offered: merging a frame takes ~1.5-1.9 s on the CPU,
                    so they would leave gaps in the trails.
   Frame limit      No limit (default) / 30 / 60 / 120 / 240 frames.
   Status           Off / Armed / Starting / Running / Reboot, with the
                    setup reminder or the last result.

RUN
 1. Close the menu and press/release SET. The camera switches to the
    full-sensor mode (about 10 s; for long shutters the first frame takes
    as long as the shutter).
 2. EXPOSURE PREVIEW: every frame is shown as exposed, nothing is recorded.
    The message shows shutter, ISO, aperture and the share of clipped
    highlights. Too bright or too dark? Half-press the shutter button to
    leave, change ISO/aperture/shutter, and press SET again.
 3. Press SET to start the composite. The frame being exposed at that
    moment is discarded; the next one is the base exposure.
 4. The LCD shows the growing composite. Press SET to stop: the exposure in
    progress is completed and merged. Or wait for the frame limit.
 5. The camera returns to normal view and saves LVFRnnnn.DNG (composite,
    camera WB as DNG white balance, EOS M focus pixels removed), .LOG and
    .CSV. Press SET again for another session; no reboot needed.

BATTERY
When the battery indicator drops to its last (red) level during a session,
the session stops like SET and the composite is saved while there is still
power for the card write ("Battery low: composite saved").

GOOD TO KNOW
 - A frame that arrives while the previous merge is still running is skipped
   (a short gap) and counted; if that keeps happening the session stops and
   saves ("Merge too slow"): use a longer shutter.
 - Around the 5th frame Canon cuts one readout short. That frame is still
   merged (the missing rows hold the previous frame) and logged.
 - The DNG holds the whole 5280-pixel-wide raster with the active area
   marked for the raw converter.

IF SOMETHING GOES WRONG
Keep the LOG/CSV/DNG from the card root: they are written at every stage. A
refusal message (wrong mode, Auto ISO, FPS override on, another RAW tool)
changes nothing: fix it and press SET again. Only if Status says "Reboot"
(RAW DMA ownership uncertain) must the camera be restarted. If the camera
ever freezes, remove the battery, then collect the LOG.
